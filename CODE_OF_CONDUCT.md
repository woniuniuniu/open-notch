# Code of Conduct

Open Notch follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and inclusive.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are
not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact method
listed on their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled
confidentially where possible.
